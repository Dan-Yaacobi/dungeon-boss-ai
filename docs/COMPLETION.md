## Final verification

- 127 unit/integration/resource tests pass, zero failures or skips.
- Five browser journeys pass, zero failed/flaky/skipped tests or captured page exceptions: fresh adventure and reload; management/mobile collection; multi-wave raid; export/import; final campaign conclusion.
- 2,160 seeded encounter simulations cover six compositions and all 20 heroes. Every tested group completed its encounters at one level above the stage recommendation.
- All 36 multi-wave raid/difficulty simulation runs complete at their tested party levels.
- The fresh campaign simulation completes 30/30 stages with earned resources, starting gems for Sylwen, affordable training, real carried health, and real shrine rules. It uses no currency injection or source edits to advance.
- A separate underleveled check confirmed that level 1/5/10/15 starter parties lose to the level-30 final boss, while progression makes the encounter attainable.
- Production and development servers return successful HTTP responses. The production benchmark records renderer identity, frame timing, JS heap diagnostics, and runtime errors in `performance-results.json`.

The hero showcase camera correction was followed by another passing fresh-adventure browser journey and a production visual check.

Machine-readable reports: `unit-results.json`, `browser-results.json`, `simulation-results.json`, `performance-results.json`. Screenshots: `town.png`, `battle.png`, `hero.png`, `mobile.png`, `raid-commander.png`, `void-boss.png`, `victory.png`.

## Limits of the evidence

The complete fresh campaign was traversed by the real engine in simulation. Browser tests cover the beginning, a full raid, persistence, and a final stage using a valid late-game fixture; a human did not manually play all 30 stages in the browser. Automated optimal play favors a forgiving campaign. Encounter pacing, subjective polish, musical quality, and broad human balance still benefit from playtesting.

The performance host uses Chromium SwiftShader software rendering. At 1280×800 the final cloud run measured 8 FPS with full shadows and 36 FPS in low mode, with no page exceptions. These software-rendered measurements do not certify 60 FPS on a gaming laptop. `?quality=low` provides a practical fallback. Desktop is the primary target; mobile collection layout is tested, while complete mobile combat and browser coverage remain limited to Chromium.

Characters and animation rigs use shared procedural construction with original silhouettes, faction features, accessories, colors, and weapons. Music is synthesized atmospheric audio. These are delivered assets, not external hand-authored asset packs. Reference sites were blocked by network policy, so no external screenshot/video inspection is claimed.

An active expedition is transient. Its full completion state is not restored after closing the browser; chest loot, finished-battle objectives, and completed-run rewards persist. This is disclosed in the game Settings and README.
